Community Transmission

The Pennsylvania Department of Health reviews community transmission in a county on a weekly basis. This is defined by ongoing transmission over multiple generations outside of the index household which cannot be explained by an outside measles introduction. Additional actions such as catch-up or early MMR vaccinations may be warranted in these counties.
